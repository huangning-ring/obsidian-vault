# Background
Former research works showed that microglia cell's disfunction, whether caused by CSF1R inhibitor or TREM2 receptor which is necessary for microglia's survive and function, will dramatically strengthen vascular calcification in Pdgfb<sup>ret/ret</sup> mice models. This pointed us that improving the TREM2 signal and activate microglia maybe help them to clear established calcification.
# AL002a antibody verification
AL002 antibody could perspectively activate TREM2. So we used Pdgfb<sup>ret/ret</sup> mouse model(blood-brain barrier leakage) again, inject flurescent labeled antibody, and then observe the flurescent's distribution. The signals have distributed to the whole brain tissue, especially the cortical areas. This result calls back to the more obvious blood-brain barrier leakage in cortical areas. 
We also observed that labeled antibody was co-located with IBA1 positive microglia, whose phosphated DPA12(pDAP12) signal dramatically strengthened. DAP12 is the downstream protein of TREM2 signal, so its phosphation can directly prove TREM2's activation. We also used negative contrast antibody and found the oppisite result. This showed us AL002 induce signal is specific for TREM2. 
The next step is to validate the treat effect. We found the cathepsin K's deposition showed increasing trend, while the contrast group's signals weren't steady. However, antibody treatment didn't bring any 改善 to calcification deposition's quality and 组成. So the conclusion is: only higher TREM2 activity isn't enough for microlia to clear established calcification.
# More fundermental verification
This negative result led to the fundermental question whether microglia could clear calcification. We refered to osteclasts, because their method to clear bone matrix is to solute hydroxyapatite at the sealed zone, then degrade the nude collagen fibers. The antibody only attached cathepsin K step, but not the acidate step, which may be the reason why calcification wasn't effectively cleared.
Although the antibody experiment's results weren't that ideal, we still found some evidence that microglia have the ability to clear calcification, which has been shown in our research to be published soon.
