# Background
Former research works showed that microglia cell's disfunction, whether caused by CSF1R inhibitor or TREM2 receptor which is necessary for microglia's survive and function, will dramatically strengthen vascular calcification in Pdgfb<sup>ret/ret</sup> mice models. This pointed us that improving the TREM2 signal and activate microglia maybe help them to clear established calcification.
# AL002a antibody verification
AL002 antibody could perspectively activate TREM2. So we used Pdgfb<sup>ret/ret</sup> mouse model(blood-brain barrier leakage) again, inject flurescent labeled antibody, and then observe the flurescent's distribution. The signals have distributed to the whole brain tissue, especially the cortical areas. This result calls back to the more obvious blood-brain barrier leakage in cortical areas.
