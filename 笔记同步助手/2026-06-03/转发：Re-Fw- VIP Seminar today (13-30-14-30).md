**EBR SEMINAR**

**MONDAY 1 June 2026**

**13:30-14:30**

**Roslin Institute Auditorium**

**Professor Tim Hore**

**University of Otago**

**New Zealand**

**Title:** **_“Unlocking the Female Ribosome and the Epigenetic Androgen Clock"_**

**Host: Dr Doug Vernimmen**

In this talk, Prof Hore will describe work on feminine ribosomal DNA (fem-rDNA), which has functionally demonstrated that ribosomal DNA is not always homogenous and universal, but can have specific functions. To do this, his laboratory created the first successful knockout of rDNA in a vertebrate (_Danio rerio_), and found it effectively blocked female differentiation, but had no other detectable effect on development. This provides a new way to understand and study heterogeneity in the most heavily transcribed genes of the genome. 

Prof Hore will also describe his group’s work on the Androgen Clock, a unique predictor of long‑term male hormone exposure in mammals, which is based on DNA methylation profiles only.
