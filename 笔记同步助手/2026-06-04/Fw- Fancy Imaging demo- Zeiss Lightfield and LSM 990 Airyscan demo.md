Hi all

  
Zeiss will be demonstrating their **Lightfield 4D imaging system** on campus, in the **Zebrafish Imaging Facility at the Chancellor’s Building**, on **the 17th**. This is a very advanced imaging system that can capture fast 3D images from thick fluorescent samples, such as zebrafish, organoids, and tissue slices. It should be a great opportunity to see how cutting-edge imaging technology works in practice.

This could also be a nice chance to visit another imaging facility on campus, meet people working with advanced microscopy, and get a feel for the imaging platforms available here. 

Also, Zeiss will be bringing Krispy Kreme donuts to the presentation, so there will be cool images, interesting technology, and sugar involved!

**Subject:** Zeiss Lightfield and LSM 990 Airyscan demo 

Dear all,

Some of you may remember Zeiss’ fancy Lightfield 4D system, which was on demo in IRR North last year. This is an imaging system that takes a 3D image of a thick fluorescent sample (e.g. a zebrafish, a series of organoids, or a thick slice of tissue) in less time it took you to read this sentence. Yes, it is that fast. Zeiss is currently selling the LightField bolted on an AiryScan confocal -I hope to see it one day available for a widefield. 

There will be a new demo of the system in the Chancellor’s building, on campus, in the Zebrafish Imaging Facility lead by Daniel Soong (see attached flyer). Those of you who are interested in testing the system or seeing it work should fill in the attached form and contact Daniel.

As the best way to see the speed of the Lightfield 4D time lapses is in person rather than online, and to compensate for your trek to the Chancellor’s, Zeiss will bring Krispy Kreme donuts to the presentation on the 17th. Cool images and sugar, what’s not to like!

Sincerely,
