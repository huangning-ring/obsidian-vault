# Opening
The author has been researching PXE for 20 years. 
PXE was by the lack of ABCC6 which almost only express in liver cells. However, the feature -- ectopic mineralization seems unreasonable at all. Why does it happen?
Why is a gene that only express in liver can cause mineralization in other tissues? We tested its transcripting level by NGS, resulting that several 激酶级联通路 can be focused on inhibition of ABCC6. 
Until 2014, the researchers found that ABCC6 can transport ATP to blood circulation, which soon be cut by ENPP1, generating PPi and AMP. We all know that PPi is a strong and effective inhibitor of calcification, so its deficiency must cause ectopic mineralization. That's the mechanism! This is the first leap in Tamás Arányi's research.
We all know that the ateral wall are constructed by 3 layers, and the calcification usually takes place in endometrium and mesentery.
Endometrium calcification is the marker of atherosclerosis while Mesentery calcification is driven by osteoblast transformation of AMCs. This is a positively regulared biology program. However, the above molecular mechanism was an important, single-factor depletion model driven by systemic PPi depletion.
