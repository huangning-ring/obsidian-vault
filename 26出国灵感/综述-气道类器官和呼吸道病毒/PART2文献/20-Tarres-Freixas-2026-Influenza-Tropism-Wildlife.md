# 文章做了什么
研究建立来自10种分类跨度较大的野生动物和家畜的气道类器官面板，分别测试哺乳动物来源pH1N1与禽源H5N1。不同宿主模型在感染率和细胞病变方面差异显著，并与已有体内资料及野外观察相符。该面板还能用于研究受体结合、病毒动力学和陌生宿主中的早期适应，为跨物种流感监测提供统一实验平台。
# 对本项目的意义
对许多野生物种开展活体感染既不现实又受伦理限制，传统动物实验难以形成广覆盖、可比较的宿主面板。物种来源气道类器官以可扩增的呼吸道组织替代部分活体挑战，直接服务于宿主嗜性和早期适应筛查。其不可替代性主要来自稀有物种取样与跨物种并行比较的可行性；能否替代具体动物结局仍需全文证据限定。
# 原始摘要
Identifying animal species that are susceptible to the plethora of existing and emerging viruses is critical for predicting and containing disease outbreaks. Current efforts to assess viral tropism largely rely on experimental infection models, but such experiments are logistically and ethically infeasible for many wildlife species. To tackle this challenge, we developed a panel of airway organoids from ten taxonomically diverse wildlife and livestock species and evaluated their susceptibility to influenza viruses of mammalian (pH1N1) and avian (H5N1) origin. Our analyses revealed large host-specific differences in infection rate and cytopathogenicity that aligned with known in vivo data and field observations. Furthermore, we demonstrated that this organoid panel can serve as a powerful tool to elucidate receptor-binding mechanisms, viral dynamics and early host adaptation in poorly characterized animal hosts. In summary, this work provides a robust and ethically viable approach for evaluating viral tropism and adaptation in wildlife species, and may aid current pandemic preparedness, zoonotic disease surveillance and wildlife conservation efforts.
