# 广州塔
> [!tip] 广州塔介绍语
Canton Tower stands over 600 meters high, making it the third highest tower globally and the tallest television tower in China. 
Its unique "slim waist" design has earned it the nickname "Little Waist." 
Visitors can enjoy an all-glass observation deck with see-through floors, indoor viewing at level 84, and an outdoor platform at 433 meters. 
The world's highest horizontal Ferris wheel is located at 450 meters. A high-speed elevator takes only 48 seconds to reach the top. 
Remember to purchase tickets in advance as queues can be long, but children enter free. 
Don't forget to capture photos of this iconic landmark!

# 珠江夜游
> [!example] 珠江夜游介绍语
Our Pearl River night cruise departs from Dashatou Wharf and lasts approximately 60 to 70 minutes. 
During this journey, we'll pass by **Haixinsha Asian Games Park, Zhujiang Beer Brewery, Xinghai Concert Hall, and Guangdong Museum of Art, with views of historic Tianzi Wharf Building.** 
The boat features air-conditioned cabins and an open-air upper deck. Free tea is provided, and boats depart every half hour. 
Please hold the railings, stay away from the edges, and keep your personal belongings secure. 
The lighting shows on both river banks create a spectacular display, especially the reflections of Canton Tower and the unique Haixin Bridge.
